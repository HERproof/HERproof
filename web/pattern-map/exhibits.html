What the software will not say It does not say what caused an injury, how old a bruise is, or that anyone hurt anyone. It says what the photograph shows, when the file says it was taken, and what else happened that day. A clinician or the court draws the rest.
Exhibit J shows a child. Whether a child's photograph goes into a filing, and whether it brings DV-105 with it, is a decision for the attorney and the person filing, never a default.
