California DVRO forms

The Judicial Council forms a domestic violence restraining order is filed on, and exactly which parts get filled from your own record. Every form links to the official PDF on the California Courts site. See the blank to filled walkthrough for where each line comes from.

What this does, and what it refuses to do
  • It does not choose your forms. This is the standard set the California Courts publish. Which ones apply to you is a legal question, and courts have local forms on top of these.
  • It does not advise. No opinion on whether to file, what orders to ask for, whether you qualify, or how a judge is likely to rule.
  • It does not conclude. No finding that abuse occurred, no risk score, no prediction.
  • It transcribes, and shows its work. Every filled field carries the file, line and timestamp it came from, so you and your attorney can check it against the original.
  • It leaves gaps as gaps. Anything not in the record stays blank rather than being guessed at.
  • You sign it, so you edit it. DV-100 is signed under penalty of perjury. Every word is yours to correct before anyone signs anything.
  • Nothing is filed. The packet goes to an attorney or a court self-help center. This software does not file, serve or appear.

The core packet

What you file to ask for the order.

FormWhat it isWhat the record fills
DV-100 Request for Domestic Violence Restraining Order. Where you tell the judge what happened and why you need the order. Signed under penalty of perjury. Item 5 and the past abuse section, quoted verbatim and dated, each line traceable to a file and a timestamp.
CLETS-001 Confidential CLETS Information. Helps police enforce the order. The restrained person never gets a copy. Left blank on purpose. No date of birth, vehicle or description appears in the record, and a guess on a law enforcement form is worse than an empty box.
DV-109 Notice of Court Hearing. The court completes most of it. Party names only. The rest is the court's.
DV-110 Temporary Restraining Order. The court completes most of it. Nothing. Which orders to request is a legal call, not the software's.
DV-120 Response to the request. Goes to the other side blank. Nothing. Served blank, as the court directs.
DV-200 Proof of Personal Service. Filled out after service. Nothing. Signed by whoever served the papers.
MC-025 Attached Declaration, for when the box on DV-100 runs out of room. The full chronology, exhibits and thread, page numbered with the file's fingerprint in the footer of every page.
FL-150 Income and Expense Declaration. Only if you are asking for support or attorney's fees. Nothing. Financial records are outside what the record holds.

Renewing later

A long-term order can run up to five years, and you can ask to renew before it expires.

FormWhat it isWhat the record fills
DV-700Request to Renew Restraining Order.Keeps the record current so the reasons are dated and quotable at renewal.
DV-710Notice of Hearing to Renew.Nothing.
Courts can have their own local forms on top of these, so the packet goes to an attorney or a court self-help center before it is filed.
