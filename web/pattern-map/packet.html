MethodHow this record was built

Where it ran

On the petitioner's own machine. The files came across by cable, were encrypted as they landed, and no copy was uploaded. The packet is sealed for the attorney's key before it is sent, so what crosses the network is ciphertext.

What Gemini did

Three jobs, and only three. It put 2,184 messages in date order using the timestamps in the files. It grouped what repeats into six kinds across 19 months. It copied the exact words into the matching box on the form, carrying the line number with each one.

What it did not do

It did not write a sentence anyone did not say. It did not score the relationship, rate risk, or reach a conclusion about what happened. It did not fill CLETS-001, DV-109, DV-110 or DV-105. It did not treat the 24 day gap as calm.

What a person still has to do

Read it, correct anything that is not how she would say it, decide what to ask the judge for, and sign it. An attorney or the court's self-help center picks it up from here.

HerProof is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. It gets the record ready for a lawyer and hands it over. Nothing in this packet has been filed or sent.
